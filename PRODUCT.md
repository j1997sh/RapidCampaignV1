# Rapid Campaign

## Product proposition

**One campaign. Every local version.**

Rapid Campaign is a standalone campaign operations platform for organisations that need to launch one campaign across many geographic areas quickly. It combines localised landing pages, forms, campaign creative, paid-media setup, Meta operations, attribution and geographic performance reporting.

It is sector-neutral. Political and public-affairs geography is supported as an optional preset, not as the product model.

## Core objects

- Campaign
- Area
- Local page
- Form / action
- Creative
- Audience reference
- Ad campaign / ad set / ad
- Response / lead
- Attribution
- Result

An **area** can be a constituency, ward, postcode area, town, city, region, branch territory, franchise territory, store catchment or any custom CSV-defined geography.

## Primary workflow

1. Create a master campaign.
2. Add areas by CSV, manual entry, or an optional geography preset.
3. Generate local campaign pages and forms.
4. Generate/match local creative.
5. Connect Meta and preflight audiences, copy, budget, URLs and creative.
6. Create PAUSED campaigns/ad sets/ads.
7. Track spend -> visits -> responses by area and variant.
